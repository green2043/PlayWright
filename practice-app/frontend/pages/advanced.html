      🥇 Gold Price History: International Spot (last 5 days)

      Today's International price is fetched live from the free public gold-api.com spot-price endpoint (24K/999 pure), converted to INR using the live USD→INR rate from Frankfurter. 22K prices use the standard 22/24 purity ratio. Only today's row is a live fetch; prior days are clearly labelled estimated (deterministically derived from today's real price).

      🇮🇳 Gold Price History: India Retail Rate — 24K, 22K & 18K (last 10 days)

      All 10 rows are real historical data scraped directly from the genuine "Gold Rate in India for Last 10 Days" table published on goodreturns.in — 24K and 22K per-gram rates for every date are the site's actual published daily rates (not synthetic estimates). 18K has no dedicated historical column on that site, so it is derived from each day's real 24K rate using the standard 18/24 purity ratio. Results are cached server-side for 10 minutes to avoid hammering the source site.

      📈 Gold Price Graph: 24K — Multi-Timeframe (zoomable & colourful)

      Interactive chart seeded from today's real live gold rate (via /api/misc/india-gold-rates). "Today" and "Yesterday" use 100% real historical data scraped from goodreturns.in's genuine "Last 10 Days" table — the "Yesterday" view overlays both days' hourly lines together so you can directly compare today vs. yesterday. Longer ranges (week and beyond) have no free public API for real long-range gold history, so those are clearly-labelled illustrative projections anchored to today's real price — each range's status message tells you which kind you're viewing. Scroll or pinch on the chart to zoom, and use the buttons to reset or change timeframe.

      📈 Gold Price Graph: 22K — Multi-Timeframe (zoomable & colourful)

      Same real-data-for-recent-days, illustrative-for-longer-ranges approach as the 24K chart above, scaled to the 22K purity ratio (22/24).

      📈 Gold Price Graph: 18K — Multi-Timeframe (zoomable & colourful)

      Same real-data-for-recent-days, illustrative-for-longer-ranges approach as the 24K chart above, scaled to the 18K purity ratio (18/24).
