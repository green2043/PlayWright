    Dynamically-Loaded Dropdown (options arrive after a delay)

    The dropdown starts disabled with a placeholder option; clicking "Load Options" simulates a network call that populates it ~1.5s later - practise Playwright's auto-waiting for the disabled attribute to clear and for new <option> elements to appear.

    Cascading (Dependent) Dropdowns

    Selecting a Continent dynamically repopulates the Country dropdown, and selecting a Country dynamically repopulates the City dropdown - each downstream dropdown resets/disables itself until its parent has a valid selection.
